# AI-Assisted Digital Production & Localization

## Overview
This case study highlights international digital-production work involving a long-form multilingual dubbing/localization workflow.

## Challenge
The production required careful handling of speaker assignments, generated voice segments, synchronization, audio matching, quality control and deadline-sensitive troubleshooting.

## Work Performed
- Worked with specialized dubbing/localization software
- Managed speaker/voice assignments
- Reviewed large numbers of dialogue lines
- Identified missing audio and timing problems
- Corrected overlaps and synchronization issues
- Distinguished dialogue from non-dialogue sound effects
- Performed repeated quality-control checks
- Worked through export and delivery troubleshooting under tight deadlines

## Skills Demonstrated
AI-assisted production • audio workflow QA • localization • attention to detail • troubleshooting • deadline management • digital production operations

## Relevance to AI Operations
The project required human judgment around AI-generated output, systematic error detection, iterative correction and quality assurance—skills that transfer directly to AI evaluation and AI operations work.

## Privacy
Source media, proprietary production assets and client-sensitive materials are not included.
