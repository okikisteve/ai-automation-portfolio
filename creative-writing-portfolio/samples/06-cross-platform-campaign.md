# One Idea, Three Platforms — Social Campaign Mini-Pack

**Campaign concept:** The best business automation is invisible when it works.  
**Brand:** Omnify AI  
**Goal:** Explain the value of lead follow-up without turning every post into a technical tutorial.

## LinkedIn — Insight and Credibility

A customer inquiry isn't just a message. It's the beginning of a business process.

Someone has to capture the details, understand the request, assign responsibility and make sure the next step happens.

When those steps depend entirely on memory or scattered chats, opportunities can slip through.

Automation can organize the routine work. People should still own the judgment, relationships and final decisions.

**Question for founders:** Where does a customer inquiry most often get stuck in your process?

## Instagram — Short, Visual, Saveable

**Carousel Slide 1:** Your lead didn't disappear. Your follow-up did.

**Slide 2:** New inquiry arrives.

**Slide 3:** Details get buried in chat.

**Slide 4:** Nobody owns the next step.

**Slide 5:** A simple workflow can log, assign and remind.

**Slide 6:** Let automation handle the handoff. Let your team handle the human conversation.

**Caption:** You don't always need more leads. Sometimes you need a better way to take care of the leads you already have. Save this if your inbox has become your CRM.

## TikTok / Reels — 20-Second Script

**0–3s — HOOK**  
**VISUAL:** Phone buzzing; unread messages stack up.  
**VOICEOVER:** “You paid to get that lead. Then forgot to reply?”

**4–10s — PROBLEM**  
**VISUAL:** Notes, chat threads and spreadsheets scattered across the screen.  
**VOICEOVER:** “The inquiry came in. The details went everywhere.”

**11–17s — SOLUTION**  
**VISUAL:** One lead record appears; follow-up assigned; reminder sent.  
**VOICEOVER:** “A simple automation can collect the details and alert the right person.”

**18–20s — CTA**  
**VISUAL:** Human agent replies to a client.  
**VOICEOVER:** “Automate the handoff. Keep the relationship human.”

---

### Adaptation Rationale

LinkedIn starts a professional discussion. Instagram breaks the idea into a visual lesson. TikTok uses an immediate tension-and-resolution structure. The campaign does not claim a specific conversion lift or guaranteed business result.
