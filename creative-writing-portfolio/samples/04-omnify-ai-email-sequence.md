# Omnify AI — Three-Email Outreach Sequence

**Format:** B2B prospecting emails  
**Audience:** Independent real estate businesses managing inquiries, property inspections and follow-ups  
**Service context:** AI-assisted lead intake, qualification and scheduling workflows  
**Goal:** Start a useful conversation—not promise unrealistic automation outcomes.

## Email 1 — The Missed Inquiry

**Subject:** What happens to property inquiries after office hours?

Hi [First Name],

A buyer finds a listing at 9:40 p.m. They have one question and want to schedule a viewing.

By morning, they may have contacted three other agents.

I work on practical automation for real estate teams: collecting inquiry details, organizing leads, routing follow-ups and helping coordinate inspection requests.

The aim isn't to replace the human relationship. It's to make sure promising conversations don't disappear into a crowded inbox.

Would a quick review of how your team handles incoming leads be useful?

Best,  
Steven  
Omnify AI

## Email 2 — The Specific Workflow

**Subject:** A simpler way to track viewing requests

Hi [First Name],

Following up with one concrete example.

Imagine every new property inquiry enters a shared lead sheet with the property requested, budget, preferred viewing time and contact details. The responsible agent receives an alert, while the prospect gets an acknowledgment.

That workflow can be designed around existing tools, with a human confirming the actual appointment.

You keep control of the customer relationship; the routine steps become easier to manage.

Would you be open to seeing a simple workflow diagram for your business?

Best,  
Steven  
Omnify AI

## Email 3 — Respectful Close

**Subject:** Should I close the loop?

Hi [First Name],

I'll keep this short.

If missed inquiries or scattered lead records are slowing your team down, I can help outline a small, practical automation pilot.

If it's not a priority right now, no problem—I won't keep filling your inbox.

Would you prefer a one-page example, or should I close the loop?

Best,  
Steven  
Omnify AI

---

### Why This Sequence Works

- **Email 1:** Opens with a believable moment of friction instead of a generic sales pitch.
- **Email 2:** Makes an abstract service tangible with a five-field workflow.
- **Email 3:** Respects the reader's time and offers a clear, easy response.
- **Guardrails:** No invented customer results, no guaranteed revenue claims, no claim that appointments are automatically confirmed without human verification.

**Status:** Original portfolio sample, not a documented live campaign.
