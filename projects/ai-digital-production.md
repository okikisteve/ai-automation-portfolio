# AI-Assisted Digital Production & Localization

[← Portfolio](../README.md)

## Project Overview
Worked on a long-form English-to-German dubbing/localization workflow requiring voice assignment, synchronization, audio review and troubleshooting.

## Production Workflow
```text
Source video/dialogue → speaker mapping → voice generation/import
                      → line alignment & synchronization
                      → audio quality checks → export review
```

## Contributions
- Reviewed dialogue and speaker assignments across a large set of lines.
- Distinguished speech from effects and other audio that should not be dubbed.
- Diagnosed missing or inaudible audio and incorrect voice assignments.
- Adjusted timing overlaps and synchronization.
- Repeatedly checked generated audio and export behavior under deadlines.

## Challenges & Lessons
Speaker-label inconsistencies, silent stretches, timing overlaps and export issues highlighted the importance of human review, traceable corrections and validation before delivery.

## Transferable Skills
AI-output quality assurance · process discipline · troubleshooting · localization operations · time-sensitive review.
