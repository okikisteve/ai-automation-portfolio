## How I Approach a Brief

1. Identify the reader or viewer, their real problem and the desired response.
2. Develop one central promise or story question.
3. Draft multiple hooks, then choose the strongest truthful angle.
4. Write for the medium: read-aloud rhythm for scripts, scan-friendly structure for websites, concise value for email.
5. Edit for clarity, specificity, cultural context, voice and a useful next action.
