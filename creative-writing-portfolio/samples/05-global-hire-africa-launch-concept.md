# Global Hire Africa — Concept Launch Copy

**Format:** Landing page and LinkedIn launch announcement  
**Project status:** Early-stage product concept; copy is illustrative, not a claim that a live platform exists.  
**Audience:** Skilled African professionals seeking credible global opportunities, and employers seeking talented candidates.

## Hero

### Talent is everywhere. Opportunity should be, too.

You have the skills. You've done the work. Finding opportunities that genuinely welcome applicants from your location shouldn't feel like a guessing game.

**Global Hire Africa** is a proposed career platform built around a clearer connection between African talent and employers hiring across borders.

**CTA (concept):** Join the Early-Access List

## The Friction

You've seen it before: a role says “remote,” but only names its country restrictions after you invest time applying.

For employers, the challenge looks different: great candidates may be overlooked because sourcing across markets feels complicated.

Both sides need better signals.

## The Proposed Experience

**For candidates**  
Discover opportunities with clearer location eligibility, organize applications and access optional AI-assisted career support.

**For employers**  
Reach a wider talent pool, communicate role requirements clearly and make candidate matching more structured.

**For everyone**  
Less guesswork. Better information. More purposeful connections.

## Call to Action

A fairer job search starts with clarity.

**Join the conversation about what cross-border hiring should look like.**

## LinkedIn Launch Concept

“Remote” should tell you where you can work—not leave you guessing.

Too many skilled professionals spend hours on opportunities that were never open to their location. Employers, meanwhile, miss strong talent because discovery stops at borders.

That's the problem we're exploring with Global Hire Africa: clearer eligibility, practical career tools and stronger connections between talent and hiring teams.

We're still shaping the idea. If you've faced this problem as an applicant or employer, what would you change first?

### Copy Decisions

The landing page speaks to the candidate's real frustration before presenting features. The language is intentionally conditional: this is a concept, and no launched service, hiring outcomes or employer partnerships are claimed.
