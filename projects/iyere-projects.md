# Iyere Projects — Lead & CRM Automation

## Business Context
Iyere Projects Limited provides interior architecture, finishing and project services. The automation project focused on making lead intake and follow-up more structured and reducing manual administrative work.

## Problem
New prospect information needed to be captured consistently, stored centrally and made easier to act on. Manual handling can create delays, missing information and inconsistent follow-up.

## Solution
I designed an n8n-based workflow that connects a structured intake form with Google Sheets and follow-up actions.

### Workflow
1. Prospect submits the lead form.
2. n8n receives and structures the submission.
3. Lead information is mapped into a Google Sheets database.
4. AI-assisted processing can support qualification and response logic.
5. Calendar integration creates scheduled follow-up events.
6. WhatsApp/API integration was explored for direct communication.
