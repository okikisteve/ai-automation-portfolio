# The Drum That Would Not Lie

**Format:** Original short animation script  
**Series concept:** African Wonder Tales  
**Characters:** Zara, Kofi, Chike (a talking lion cub), Tutu (a talking tortoise)  
**Audience:** Children and families  
**Theme:** Tell the truth early; teamwork turns mistakes into solutions.

## Scene 1 — The Missing Rain Drum

**EXT. VILLAGE SQUARE — MORNING**

A clay drum sits beneath the oldest baobab tree. The villagers strike it to call everyone together when the rains are late.

**ZARA:** The clouds are coming! We should call the village before the first drop.

**KOFI:** I'll get the drumstick!

He dashes away. Chike races after him, paws kicking up dust.

**CHIKE:** First one there wins a mango!

**TUTU:** A race? How exciting. I'll arrive on Thursday.

Everyone laughs.

Kofi grabs the drumstick and spins it like a baton. It slips from his hand, hits the drum, and cracks its side.

**SFX:** *CRRRK.*

Kofi freezes.

**KOFI (whispering):** Maybe no one will notice.

He hides the broken side beneath a bright cloth.

## Scene 2 — A Quiet Sound

Zara tries to call the villagers. She strikes the drum.

**SFX:** *Thup.*

**ZARA:** That doesn't sound right.

**CHIKE:** Perhaps the drum has a sore throat.

**TUTU:** Or perhaps somebody knows more than the drum is telling us.

Kofi looks away. Rain begins to patter on the leaves.

**ZARA:** We can't wait. The stream rises quickly when it rains. People need to move their baskets and goats away from the bank.

Kofi opens his mouth, but worry closes it again.

## Scene 3 — The Truth Travels Faster

Tutu places a gentle foot beside Kofi's shoe.

**TUTU:** A mistake is a heavy stone. The truth lets us put it down.

**KOFI:** I broke the drum. I was showing off. I thought everyone would be angry.

**ZARA:** I'm worried about the rain, not about blame. Thank you for telling us.

Chike lifts his head.

**CHIKE:** Then we need another sound!

He roars. It comes out as a tiny squeak.

**CHIKE:** I'm still working on the big version.

Zara spots two cooking pots near the market. She taps one with a wooden spoon. Its clear ring carries across the square.

**ZARA:** Kofi, take the other pot to the bridge. Chike, run to the fields. Tutu, can you tell the elders?

**TUTU:** I shall speak slowly and very, very loudly.

## Scene 4 — A Better Call

**MONTAGE:** Zara rings the market pot. Kofi echoes from the bridge. Chike delivers the message to the fields. Tutu announces it to the elders.

Villagers gather their baskets, guide the goats uphill and share shelter as the rain begins.

The old drummer examines the cracked clay.

**DRUMMER:** We can mend a drum. I'm glad you told the truth in time.

Kofi looks at the repaired village square, then at his friends.

**KOFI:** Next time, I'll tell the truth before the rain.

**CHIKE:** And next time, I shall roar like a lion!

He tries again. Another squeak.

**TUTU:** Splendid. By Thursday, you'll be terrifying.

**END CARD:** *A mistake can be repaired. A hidden mistake can grow.*

---

### Creative Notes

The story uses a simple four-scene structure, distinct character voices and humor without undermining the lesson. Dialogue is short enough for young listeners, and each scene gives animators a clear action and visual objective.
